Alina Pfrang

Postdoctoral Fellow Stanford Graduate School of Business Stanford Tax Lab

I am a postdoctoral fellow at Stanford Graduate School of Business and the Stanford Tax Lab.

My research examines how firms respond to tax policies, with a particular focus on new regulations and taxes beyond income taxation. I study when tax policies encourage new investment and business activity and when firms instead relocate activity or adjust their reporting. I also examine how tax obligations and their administration affect firms' business decisions and compliance.

Research interests
Real effects of business taxation · Environmental tax policy · Tax reporting · Anti-tax avoidance
