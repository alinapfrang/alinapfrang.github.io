Postdoctoral Fellow Stanford Graduate School of Business STax Initiative · Stanford University

Welcome. I am a postdoctoral fellow at Stanford GSB and the Stanford Tax Initiative. My research sits at the intersection of accounting and public economics, with a particular interest in how the design of tax rules — including anti-avoidance regulation, regulatory simplification, and environmental and clean energy taxes — shapes firm and individual behavior.

Research interests
Tax accounting · Anti-tax avoidance · Regulatory simplification · Environmental and clean energy taxation · Firm behavior
